The feedback loop

The pipeline doesn’t just classify entries. It classifies entries, audits the classifications, finds the patterns in its own mistakes, fixes the prompts that caused them, and then measures whether the fixes worked—all within a single session.

64% to 91% is not an incremental improvement. It’s the difference between a system that needs human review on every third entry and one that gets nine out of ten right. And the fix was three sentences added to two prompt documents.
